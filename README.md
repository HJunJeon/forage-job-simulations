My completed work from two virtual job simulations on [Forage](https://www.theforage.com), both finished in October 2026.

Author: Jun Jeon ([portfolio](https://github.com/HJunJeon) · [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/hjunjeon))

| Simulation | Focus | What's here |
|---|---|---|
| [Goldman Sachs: Operations](goldman-sachs-operations/) | Wealth management operations, trade settlement, KYC/AML, operational risk | Trade Fail Report (PDF and Word), certificate |
| [Deloitte Australia: Data Analytics](deloitte-data-analytics/) | Data analysis and forensic technology | Tableau workbook and dashboard, Python check script, classified pay equality table, certificate |

The task briefs, templates and raw datasets belong to Forage and the companies that wrote the simulations, so they are not included. The clients, factories and transactions in them are fictional.
